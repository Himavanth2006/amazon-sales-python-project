## Business Impact

* Conducted comprehensive exploratory data analysis on Amazon product data to uncover trends in pricing, discounts, customer ratings, and product popularity.
* Identified key customer engagement patterns to better understand factors influencing product performance.
* Evaluated the effectiveness of discount strategies and their impact on customer perception and product visibility.
* Provided actionable insights that can support pricing optimization, product positioning, and marketing decision-making.
* Developed data-driven recommendations through statistical analysis and visualization techniques.

## Key Findings

* Product pricing is concentrated in the affordable segment, while a limited number of premium products contribute significantly to overall revenue potential.
* Discounts are widely adopted across products, highlighting their importance as a customer acquisition and sales-driving strategy.
* Customer ratings remain consistently high across most products, indicating strong customer satisfaction and product quality.
* Customer engagement is highly concentrated among a small subset of products, suggesting opportunities to improve visibility for lower-performing products.
* The wide variety of product categories demonstrates Amazon's diverse product portfolio and varying market demand across segments.
